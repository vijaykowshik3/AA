Arpita Sharma — Founder & Principal Architect

The studio

Founded by Arpita Sharma at twenty-two, Atelier Astil has grown into a practice trusted with some of the most demanding briefs in the region — and the most personal ones.

We take a holistic approach to every space: function and aesthetics in balance, sustainability as a given, and a design language that is unmistakably ours yet always the client’s. From ultra-luxury residences for private clients to tech parks, schools and commercial environments, each project is a chance to build something rare.
