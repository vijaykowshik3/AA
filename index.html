    The studio

    Founded by Arpita Sharma at twenty-two, grown into a practice trusted with the region’s most demanding briefs — and its most personal ones.

    We take a holistic approach to every space: function and aesthetics in balance, sustainability as a given, and a design language that is unmistakably ours yet always the client’s. From ultra-luxury residences to tech parks, schools and commercial environments, each project is a chance to build something rare.
